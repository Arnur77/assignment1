About Alan Turing

Alan Turing (1912–1954) was a British mathematician and computer scientist. He made important contributions to theoretical computer science and artificial intelligence. During World War II, he worked at Bletchley Park and contributed to codebreaking. His work helped shape modern ideas about computation and computers.

Learn more Turing resources

Important Events

  1. 1912 — Born in London, England.
  2. 1936 — Published his work on computable numbers and the Turing machine.
  3. 1939 — Joined the codebreaking work at Bletchley Park.
  4. 1945 — Worked on early computer design after World War II.
  5. 1950 — Published an influential paper on machine intelligence and the imitation game.
  6. 1954 — Died in Wilmslow, England.

Key Contributions

  • Theoretical model of computation known as the Turing machine
  • Foundations of computer science
  • Important wartime codebreaking work
  • Early work related to artificial intelligence
